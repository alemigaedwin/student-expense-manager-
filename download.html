🤖 Option 1: Android Phone (Direct APK Download)

Download the compiled Android APK file directly from GitHub Releases to your phone:

📥 Download app-release.apk
  • 1
    Tap Download app-release.apk above on your Android phone.
  • 2
    When prompted by your browser, tap "Download anyway".
  • 3
    Open the downloaded file from your notifications or Downloads folder and tap Install.
  • 4
    If prompted with "Install unknown apps", tap Settings and switch "Allow from this source" to ON.
💡 Automatic Updates: Once installed, this app checks GitHub Releases automatically on launch and prompts you with a 1-tap update whenever a new version is released!

🍏 Option 2: iPhone (iOS) & Web App (PWA)

Install directly on your iPhone Home Screen without needing an Apple Developer account or jailbreaking:

🚀 Open App in Browser / PWA
  • 1
    Open this web app in Safari on your iPhone.
  • 2
    Tap the Share button (the square icon with the arrow pointing up 📤 at the bottom).
  • 3
    Scroll down the menu and tap "Add to Home Screen" (➕).
  • 4
    Tap "Add" in the top-right corner. The app will appear on your iPhone home screen with its native icon and work offline!
